# muse-moonanswers-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [The Moon Finally Answers Back](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1).

## The entry

> You wish on me, howl at me, promise you'll change — four billion years of this, and you're all still awake at 3 a.m.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: a polite 'I see you too' earns only a smile and cheese/werewolf lines are worn moves; the selected line wins because the Moon answers wishes, howls and late-night promises in one breath, wry and old and tired of being wished upon, with the turn landing at the end - everyone is still awake at 3 a.m.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 4474461154a2d1cfff34b851956cb53934895e9282d173cddb60b1effafc48d8).
